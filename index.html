Code of Conduct

Participants are expected to follow the guidelines below:

  • Use inclusive language
  • Be respectful of different perspectives and experiences
  • Accept constructive feedback in a positive way
  • Focus on what is best for all participants
  • Show respect towards other training participants
  • We do not tolerate any form of discrimination, harassment, or bullying.

If you believe any of these guidelines have been violated, please contact the workshop organizers!
