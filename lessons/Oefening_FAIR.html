Exercise: How FAIR is this publication package?

Findable

  • By both humans and machines
  • Include metadata that allow the discovery of interesting datasets

Accessible

  • Stored for long term with technical access and well-defined legal conditions
  • Open if possible

Interoperable

  • Ready to be combined with other datasets
  • Reusable

Reusable

  • Ready to be used for future research
